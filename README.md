# Parent Communicator 42

Also known as the PC42, this device allows my parents my parents to call me downstairs for whatever purpose they need. My desk is upstairs in my room and my dining table/kitchen/ living room are all below my room. whenever my parents need me for a meal or if they just want to spend time with me, they need to yell from their floor up to my room. This impacts their voice and often times, the need to call me multiple times because sometimes I don't hear them or I need a few minutes. My device lets them click a button downstairs that triggers a loud and obnoxious alarm that keeps ringing until I go downstairs and turn the alarm off on the parent module. My parents can also opt to use a PA system in order to communicate a message to me like "Go take the dog on a walk" or something similar so I know what I am being called for! There are two modules to this. The Parent module(P module) that is downstairs and is for my parents and the Child module(C module) which is for me and is in my room. The P and C modules have their own unique features:

## P Module features
* PA system
* Alarm Button
* Child turn off button
* LED do not disturb indicator

## C Module fatures
* Speaker
* Do not disturb toggle
* Snooze button(stops working after snoozing a certain amount of times so I actually go down)



The PCB is completely modular and only the neccessary parts can be plugged in for each module. This minimizes PCB cost :). The PCB is also hooked up to a 1200mAh LiPo battery and is USBC chargable.
